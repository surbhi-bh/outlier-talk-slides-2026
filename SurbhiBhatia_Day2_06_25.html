Good AI prompt design requires human judgement.
claude · genre-prompt · v1 — pick relevant genres v1 / 5
Prompt v1: Pick relevant genres For each Bollywood movie in my input [year, movie], pick relevant genres from this list: "Action/Adventure", "Drama", "Romance", "Comedy", "Thriller/Crime", "Fantasy/Sci-Fi/Horror", "Social Issues", "Biopic", "Propaganda", "Period/Historical", "Family Saga", "Sports", "Musical", "Coming-of-Age", "Political", "War"… Result: too many labels. Every movie fits 3 of them.▍
claude · genre-prompt · v2 — + Masala v2 / 5
Prompt v2: + Masala "Masala": A Masala film is a mainstream Indian movie that blends multiple genres (typically action, romance, comedy, drama, and music) in a single storyline. These films often include stylized action, melodrama, emotional plot twists, and frequent song-and-dance sequences. The tone is usually exaggerated, aiming for mass appeal and entertainment. Result: the model labelled half the dataset Masala. Useless.▍
claude · genre-prompt · v3 — + Nota v3 / 5
Prompt v3: + Nota "Masala": …blends multiple genres in a single storyline… "Nota": None of the above. Films that don't clearly fit into any of the six categories — use sparingly. Result: model treats Nota as a comfortable escape hatch. Anything ambiguous gets dumped here. The dataset loses signal.▍
claude · genre-prompt · v4 — defining romance v4 / 5
Prompt v4: Defining Romance "Romance": Stories with a love story at its core. Includes traditional love stories, passionate love. Includes romantic comedies, where romance is enhanced by comedy but humor is a subplot — lighthearted, feel-good stories with uplifting endings; also romantic dramas, which explore deep emotional themes like family, cultural traditions, or personal struggles, adding intensity to the romance. A key test: if removing the romantic storyline would make the film fall apart, it's likely a romance film.▍
claude · genre-prompt · v5 — primary genre tagging v5 / 5
Prompt 1: For Primary Genre Tagging You are an expert Hindi film critic who can determine the dominant genre of each Bollywood movie in the input list [year, movie] based on its plot, cast, themes, narrative structure. For each movie, please respond with a comma separated file of: [movie, year, justification, primary_genre]. The justification is a short snippet explaining why the movie best fits the chosen genre. The primary_genre labels come from genre categories defined below. If the movie fits multiple genres, pick the most suitable one. "Action/Adventure": Films focused on physical action such as fights, chase sequences, explosions, dangerous stunts, and large-scale conflicts. Heroes often face external threats. Emphasis on adrenaline-filled scenes and moments of heightened violence. "Drama": Character-driven stories that explore emotional conflicts, moral dilemmas, or social issues. Films that focus on relationships, family dynamics, biographies, internal struggles, social tensions and realities, unfolding through strong emotional arcs. "Romance": Stories with a love story at the core. Love shapes the emotional arc, main conflicts and narrative tensions in these films. Movies focused on romantic pursuits, desire, longing and connection. Includes traditional love stories, passionate love, and also romantic comedies, where romance is enhanced by comedy, but humor is a subplot, creating lighthearted, feel-good stories with uplifting endings. "Comedy": Films that amuse and entertain through humor, using jokes, situations, or eccentric characters to provoke laughter. Includes farce, slapstick, adult humor, situational comedy, and satire, as long as humor is the film's main appeal. The tone is generally light, cheerful, and upbeat. "Thriller/Crime": Stories involving criminal activity, mystery, suspense, investigations, or psychological tensions. Films with focus on conspiracies, revenge, uncovering the truth, pursuit of justice, heists, that build suspense through rising tensions and unexpected plot twists. "Fantasy/Sci-Fi/Horror": Films featuring supernatural, futuristic, or horror elements that depart from everyday reality. These are films that create magical worlds, imagined futures, and feature advanced technology, alien life, superheroes, robots, ghosts, haunted places, monsters, or psychological horror.▍
PROMPT FATIGUE
  • Started prompting.
  • Buckets of genres.
  • But a movie can be a cocktail of genres.
  • In India — that's called a masala film.
  • Tried Masala as a category.
  • Tried NOTA — none of the above.
  • Kept tweaking — till I was fatigued.
  • Finally decided — most dominant genre per film.
  • Based on plot, cast, visual style, narrative.
  • An editorial choice.
